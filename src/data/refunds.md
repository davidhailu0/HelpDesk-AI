# Refund and Cancellation Policy

## Section 1: Overview

We strive to ensure our customers are satisfied. Refund requests are evaluated individually based on usage and issue type.

## Section 2: 7-Day Refund Window

Customers are eligible for a **full refund within 7 days** of purchase if the product fails to meet advertised expectations and support cannot resolve the issue.

## Section 3: Partial Refunds

After 7 days and up to **30 days**, a **partial refund** may be granted depending on usage volume and plan type.  
Requests after 30 days are not eligible for refunds.

## Section 4: Subscription Cancellations

Users can cancel their subscription anytime from their account dashboard.  
Cancellation stops future billing but does not automatically trigger a refund unless within the refund window.
